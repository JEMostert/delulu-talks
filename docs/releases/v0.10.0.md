# Delulu Talks v0.10.0

## Your words, your final intent

**Spoken corrections** removes requests or phrases you explicitly take back while keeping the remaining wording. Enable it in Quick settings or Settings → Rewriting. It uses your local writing model, preserves the original transcript, and sends nothing when cleanup cannot produce a valid result. Automatic cleanup applies to prose dictation; manual rewrite previews offer the same style.

The model proposes exact deletions instead of rewriting your message. This helps preserve your voice, though ambiguous corrections still need review. Activating the feature uses balanced memory, loading speech and writing in turn. A writing runtime/model must be installed under Models; the existing Qwen3.5-2B option was used for live verification.

## Update

Finish recording before installing. Windows installer and Linux AppImage users can update in Settings → Application. Package/archive users should replace the matching installation. On Apple Silicon, quit the app and replace it using the DMG; macOS builds remain unsigned and use manual updates. Keep your application-data folder: settings, history and downloaded models are preserved.

## Validation and limits

Local build/type checks, existing unit/Python/browser checks, isolated Electron smoke, browser appearance/control review, GTK indicator previews and live Qwen3.5-2B correction checks were performed. CPU verification is not a latency benchmark. Physical microphone input and cross-application paste were not exercised end to end on every platform. Correction accuracy is model-dependent; retain/review the original when the intended change is ambiguous.
