# Delulu Talks v0.5.0

A usability pass over the whole app: every feature now lives in one place, and switching it on is next to setting it up. The full review is in [docs/usability-review.md](../usability-review.md).

- **Visible navigation:** a labeled dock sits around the record button on Home and on every page: **History**, **Words**, **Voice** and **Settings**. Quick settings is gone; its controls are in Settings.
- **Engines is folded into Settings:** the speech model is at the top of **Settings → Dictation**, next to the microphone, language and shortcut. Loading, unloading, repair, runtimes, storage and diagnostics moved to **Settings → Advanced**; models load when they are needed.
- **Rewrite in one card:** **Settings → Rewrite** sets up the model and, right below, holds **Rewrite every dictation**, the style and the model. Before, the model could be installed and loaded while the switch that uses it sat elsewhere.
- **Rewrite dialog:** style and **Rewrite** come first and the style starts on the one you chose in Settings. Context, examples and timings are under **More options**.
- **Files in History:** Bottles is gone. Use the import button in History or drop audio and video anywhere on it. One list shows files still in progress; they start transcribing right away instead of waiting in a paused queue.
- **Voice page:** one **Siren** switch also opens the agent connection, and switching it on sets up the voice if needed. **Connect your agents** shows setup steps and connected agents. **Sonar** lists what it still needs (speech recognition, Siren, a voice) with a button for each, instead of starting and never answering. Preview works whenever a voice is installed.
- **Plain names:** Logbook is History again and holds what was Bottles, Engines moved into Settings, and Lingo is Words. Siren, Sonar and Pilot keep their names inside Voice. Saved settings, page ids over IPC and the `delulu-voice` MCP/CLI contract are unchanged.
