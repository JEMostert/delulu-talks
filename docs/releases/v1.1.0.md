# Delulu Talks v1.1.0

Dictation now cleans up after itself. A small language model on your own computer tidies every transcript, learns the names you use, and remembers what you were talking about. Everything still runs locally; nothing you say leaves your machine.

- **Text cleanup:** after Whisper, Gemma 4 E4B removes filler words and stutters, drops phrases you took back ("no sorry, Tuesday"), and adds punctuation and capitals, in Dutch and English. It takes about 0.2 seconds on the graphics card. If anything goes wrong, the raw text is pasted, so dictation never waits on it. History keeps the original behind a **Cleaned up** toggle.
- **Topics:** cleanup names what each dictation is about, such as programming, gaming or finance, and uses that to fix words that were clearly misheard: "merch conflict" becomes "merge conflict" and "nerve it" becomes "nerf it". History shows the topic next to each transcript.
- **Smart context management:** the next dictation hands Whisper that topic's vocabulary and your last sentence, so names come out right from the start. It is forgotten after 10 quiet minutes, on restart, or with **Clear topic** in the tray menu. Turn it off in **Settings → Dictation**.
- **Vocabularies:** the new **Cleanup** page keeps shelves of names and terms you say, shown as glass jars on a reef. Put "T3 Code" on a Programming shelf and "ghthree code" comes out as "T3 Code". Three starter shelves are one click away: Programming, Gaming & Discord, and Twitch & Streaming. Click a jar to see, add or remove its words.
- **A new look:** the whole app is now underwater. Light falls from the surface, panels are frosted sea glass with a mother-of-pearl rim, and the pearl on Home is unchanged.
- **The recording pill, reborn:** it pops in on a spring, strings your voice as a row of iridescent pearls, sends out ripples and bubbles while you talk, spins its nacre while it thinks, and bursts into tiny pearls when the words land. It now appears on the screen you are working on, above the panel, and a little larger.
- **Smaller fixes:** the microphone level meter reacts to normal speech instead of barely moving, and setup and Settings no longer assume GNOME (KDE Plasma works the same way).

**Download size:** cleanup needs a one-time 5.2 GB model. New installs download it in the background after setup; existing installs show a **Download** button on the Cleanup page instead of starting a large download on their own. Turning cleanup off frees about 3.6 GB of graphics memory.

**Install:** the `.rpm` (Fedora), `.pkg.tar.zst` (Arch/CachyOS) or `.deb` below. It needs a Vulkan driver and, for the pill, XWayland.
