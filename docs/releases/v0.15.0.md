# Delulu Talks v0.15.0 — Word packs and simpler personalization

**Word packs.** Personalization now starts with ready-made word packs you switch on with one toggle: **Programming** (TypeScript, GitHub, kubectl, PostgreSQL, …) and **Gaming** (GG, AFK, clutch, cooldown, Valorant, …, for voice chat such as Discord). On R2T2 with an NVIDIA GPU the words are recognition hints. On every engine, known misspellings are fixed afterwards: "git hub" becomes GitHub, "cube control" kubectl, "a f k" AFK, and lowercase names such as "postgresql" get their proper capitals. Ordinary words that happen to be names, such as "steam" or "discord", are left alone.

**150 words at a time.** Packs and your own words share a budget of 150 words, shown on the page. A pack that doesn't fit can't be switched on until you make room, and the page says how many words it needs. Your own words always come first.

**Adding your own words is one field.** Type a name or term and press Add word. Optionally list how it tends to be written wrong. Language scopes, alias lists and the per-rule preview are gone from the editor; existing rules keep working. Text shortcuts are a trigger and the text to insert.

**Profiles are removed.** The saved settings profiles and their activation preview are gone, including the Profiles section in Settings. Your current settings stay as they are; saved profile data is dropped when the app starts.

**Automatic language with mixed speech.** R2T2 reports no language for Dutch full of English terms. With Automatic detection, Delulu now tells Dutch from English by the text in that case, so spoken formatting commands and language-specific rules still apply. The reported-language metadata stays unknown.

Verification: TypeScript checks, 81 unit tests, the Python suite, five browser tests and the production build passed locally. With the real R2T2 model on an RTX 4090 and synthesized Dutch and English clips, the packs improved terms such as "teammate", "clutch", "ranked", "PostgreSQL and Redis" and "OP", and left sentences without jargon unchanged, also with a full 150-word prompt. A synthetic Dutch voice mispronounces many English terms, so results with your own voice will differ; this is not a recognition-accuracy benchmark.
