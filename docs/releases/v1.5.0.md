# Delulu Talks v1.5.0

Delulu FM now makes its music on this computer by itself: no ComfyUI, no Comfy
CLI, no Python. Your songs, history and settings carry over.

- **Native music engine.** YuE2 runs in `delulu-music`, a helper shipped with the
  app that runs the model on your graphics card with Vulkan (ggml). It loads one
  part of the model at a time and exits after each song, so its graphics memory is
  free between songs. On an RTX 5060 Ti a 2:38 song took 2:47 to make, peaking at
  3.7 GB of graphics memory; its output matches ComfyUI's own YuE2 closely (see
  `docs/benchmarks/yue2-native`).
- **Settings → Models.** The music model (YuE2 3B, 4 GB, CC BY-NC 4.0) is an
  explicit download with progress and Cancel, checked against its SHA-256, and can
  be deleted again. Nothing downloads on its own, and dictation never needs it.
  Deleting removes only this app's own copy.
- **Dictation first.** The music engine pauses while you dictate and continues
  afterwards. Stopping the station stops a song in the making at once; its words
  are kept and it is composed again when you press Play.
- **Better songs.** Each song gets a real form chosen for its genre, length and
  vocals: a hook that comes back, contrast between parts, room to breathe, dense
  verses for rap and no words at all for instrumentals. Section tags no longer run
  into the line before them, and the lyrics show one line per line.
- **Fixed:** playback stopped with "resample: Insufficient buffer size 0 for input
  channel 0, expected 1024" on tracks whose length was a whole number of resampler
  blocks. A song cut at its length limit now fades out instead of stopping
  mid-phrase, and the Delulu FM page no longer has a paragraph under its heading.
