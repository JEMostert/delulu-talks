# Delulu Talks v1.2.0

Choose your speech engine: Whisper or Parakeet.

- **Parakeet:** NVIDIA's Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 is now a second speech engine. It runs on the processor, needs no graphics memory and turns a short dictation into text in about 150 ms. It recognizes 25 European languages on its own and writes punctuation and capitals. Whisper stays the default and is still the more accurate choice for short Dutch phrases.
- **Pick at setup, switch anytime:** the last setup step asks Whisper or Parakeet and downloads only that one (574 MB or 671 MB). In **Settings → Dictation** you can switch and download the other; both stay on disk, so switching back is instant.
- **Cleanup with Parakeet:** Parakeet doesn't report the language, so Delulu Talks works it out from the text and cleanup still runs for Dutch and English. A fixed language in Settings overrides the guess.
- **Model switching fix:** choosing a model that isn't downloaded yet no longer unloads the one you're using or sends you back to setup. Dictation keeps working until the new download finishes.

Also in this release, interface fixes that were planned for v1.1.3:

- **Cleanup download:** the download bar, Ready check and Try again button now look like the speech model's, instead of a plain progress bar and bare text.
- **Cleaned up toggle:** in Recent and History you can now see whether you're looking at the cleaned text or what you said, and Insert again inserts the one that is shown.
- **Vocabulary jars:** words no longer hide behind a two-line shelf name, and starter and new-shelf jars keep their outline in dark mode.
- **Small things:** About shows the right version, renaming a shelf keeps keyboard focus in the dialog, setup points to the Cleanup page for turning cleanup off, and the window can no longer be shrunk until the layout breaks.

**Install:** the `.rpm` (Fedora), `.pkg.tar.zst` (Arch/CachyOS) or `.deb` below. It needs a Vulkan driver and, for the pill, XWayland.
