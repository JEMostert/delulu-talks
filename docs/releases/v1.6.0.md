# Delulu Talks v1.6.0

Dictation gets the language right, types nothing for noise and keeps long
recordings whole. History moves into a searchable database. Delulu FM gets a
second, much faster music engine and a native host voice. Your history, songs
and settings carry over.

## Dictation

- **Whisper fixes.** A dictation now comes out in the language you spoke, also
  right after one in your other language. Noise, typing and breathing no longer
  turn into text: a small speech detector (Silero VAD, MIT, built in) checks
  each recording first. Recordings over 30 seconds keep all their text, and a
  short Dutch clip is no longer replaced by "Dank u wel." Speed is unchanged.
- **Your languages.** With Language on Automatic, dictation only picks from your
  languages (Dutch and English unless you change them in Settings → Dictation),
  so short or quiet clips no longer come out as Icelandic or Czech.
- **Faster cleanup.** Cleanup of a 25-word dictation is about 28% faster (588 →
  420 ms on an RTX 5060 Ti), with the same output.
- **New recording pill.** The pill is now its own small program, `delulu-pill`:
  about 4.5 MB of memory instead of 66 MB, on screen 13 ms after the key press,
  and sharp and correctly sized at 150% and 200% scaling on KDE. The old pill
  stays as a fallback.
- **No dictated text in the journal.** Each dictation logs timings and lengths
  only; start the app with `DELULU_LOG_TEXT=1` if you want the text there for
  debugging. A dictation that History cannot save is still typed or copied.

## History

- **History and vocabulary live in one SQLite database** with fast search that
  ignores case and accents and matches any part of a word. The move happens once,
  at the first start, and keeps every entry; the old files stay next to it as
  `history.jsonl.v1.bak` and `vocabulary.json.v1.bak`.
- **Retention.** Keep history forever (the default) or for 90, 30 or 7 days.
- **Deleting is real deletion:** a deleted entry is also removed from the
  database file and from the old backup.

## Delulu FM

- **ACE-Step 1.5 as a second music engine.** Choose it in the Music engine panel
  on the Delulu FM page. A song takes about 26–61 seconds on an RTX 5060 Ti,
  against a few minutes with YuE2. YuE2 stays the default. ACE-Step has been
  tested on NVIDIA only.
- **Native host voice.** The host speaks with Supertonic 3 on the CPU, in Dutch
  for a Dutch station and in English otherwise, with no Python. `delulu speak`
  uses the same voice. Install it in Settings → Voice replies (401 MB, with its
  licence shown first), and pick the voices there.
- **Smaller station.** Songs and host breaks are stored as Ogg Opus (about a
  tenth of the size), and every item plays at an even loudness. Existing WAV
  files are converted in the background after start; each WAV is removed only
  after its Opus copy is verified.

## Downloads and install

- **Model downloads resume** after a dropped connection, Cancel or a restart,
  and check free disk space before they start. Music models download and delete
  one by one.
- **One GPU helper.** `delulu-engine` replaces `delulu-cleanup` and
  `delulu-music`, about 39 MB less on disk.

## Known issue

- On GNOME, the first dictation after the app starts can sometimes paste what
  was on the clipboard before instead of the dictated text (#531). History has
  the right text. A fix is in progress.
