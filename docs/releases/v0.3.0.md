# Delulu Talks v0.3.0

- **Talk:** one switch on the Voice page starts a live conversation loop with your coding agents. Each sentence is routed on its own as soon as it is transcribed, with no waiting and no merging of turns. Next to the switch: a live microphone level meter and a microphone picker.
- **Local helper as a tool caller:** with Lightweight · 0.8B selected, every turn becomes one action. It can forward your exact words to an agent (or as a follow-up on its task), report status, cancel after a spoken yes, stop speaking, stop listening, repeat the last line, or answer in one short sentence in your language (English or Dutch). The helper decides in about 0.3 s on an NVIDIA GPU. Turning Talk on loads it from cached files; only the first download asks.
- **Helper Off** uses the same loop: everything you say goes to your agent, word for word.
- **One agent inbox:** every agent session gets its own id, and every call counts as a heartbeat. Agents silent for two minutes stop receiving tasks, and Delulu tells you nobody is listening instead of queueing silently. Storage is now an append-only log instead of rewriting the whole file on every acknowledgement.
- **Spoken briefings wait their turn:** when an agent finishes, gets stuck or fails, Delulu says one short line at the next pause and never talks over you or another message. Several updates become one line.
- **Speech starts sooner:** the first sentence is synthesized and played on its own.
- **Breaking for agents:** the `listen …` commands and tools are gone. Agents use `agent register` / `agent next` / `task update`. Reinstall the skill from the Voice page.
- The 2B helper is no longer offered; a saved Conversational choice now uses Lightweight.

Details, eval scores and what still needs a live microphone test: [voice-redesign.md](../voice-redesign.md#overnight-results).
