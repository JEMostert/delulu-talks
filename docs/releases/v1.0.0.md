# Delulu Talks v1.0.0

A fresh start. Delulu Talks is now a small, local push-to-talk dictation app for Linux: hold a hotkey, speak Dutch or English, let go, and the text is typed into the app you were using. It was rebuilt from scratch in Rust, with no Python and no runtime installer.

- **Dictation only, done well:** speech is recognised on your graphics card with whisper.cpp (Large v3 Turbo through Vulkan). The language is detected automatically; **Settings → Dictation → Language** fixes it to one language if you want. From letting go of the key to the text appearing usually takes well under a second.
- **Hotkey:** hold **Super+Z** to talk, or tap it once to start and again to stop. GNOME owns the shortcut; **Settings → Input → Hotkey → Change…** opens it there.
- **Typing into other apps:** the text is copied and pasted with **Shift+Insert** through the desktop's remote-desktop permission. **Settings → Dictation → Insert text with** switches to Ctrl+V, Ctrl+Shift+V or copy only. Every transcript is saved to History before it is pasted, so nothing is lost if pasting fails.
- **Recording pill:** the pearl is back. While you dictate, a small click-through pill at the bottom of the screen glows with your voice and breathes while it transcribes. It never takes focus. Turn it off in **Settings → Appearance**.
- **First run:** three steps (microphone, permissions, model download), then done. On GNOME the second step shows two system dialogs: one for the shortcut and one for typing into other apps.
- **New look:** Home, History and Settings were redesigned around the pearl, in light and dark. Every setting lives in exactly one place.

**Install:** the `.rpm` (Fedora), `.pkg.tar.zst` (Arch/CachyOS) or `.deb` below. It needs a Vulkan driver and, for the pill, XWayland.

**Known limit:** very short Dutch phrases (two to four words) are sometimes taken for German. If that bites, set **Settings → Dictation → Language** to Dutch.

**Not carried over from 0.x:** settings, history and word lists start fresh. Rewrite and voice are not in 1.0; they come back later as separate steps. AppImage and the auto-updater are gone.
