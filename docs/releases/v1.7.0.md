# Delulu Talks v1.7.0

The window has a new layout: a sidebar, pages that fill the window, and far less
text. Delulu FM picks a random genre, mood and length for every song from a mix
you set once. Parakeet Ultra is a second speech engine, and speech recognition
runs outside the app. Your history, vocabularies, songs and settings carry over.

## New layout

- **Sidebar.** Dictate, Vocabulary, Delulu FM, Voice, Models and Settings sit in
  a sidebar. Every page uses the whole window, and only lists scroll. The
  sidebar shows what Delulu FM is playing, with pause and skip, on every page.
- **Dictate** replaces Home and History. The pearl and every dictation setting
  (hotkey, microphone, engine, language, cleanup, smart context, paste keys,
  recording pill) are on the left; the history is on the right, one line per
  dictation, with search and Today, Week and All. Click a line for the full
  text.
- **Models** lists every model in one table: speech, cleanup, voice and music,
  with what uses it, its size and one button to use, download or delete it.
- **Settings** holds only what belongs to the whole app: theme, start at login,
  which features show, and About.
- **Vocabulary** (was Cleanup) shows your shelves in a list with the open
  shelf's terms beside it.
- **Less text.** Help paragraphs became tooltips. Licence text is out of the
  pages: each model has a licence tag that opens its licence, and Settings →
  About → Licences lists them all. The voice model still shows its terms before
  it downloads.

## Delulu FM

- **The mix.** Switch on the genres and moods you like; each song draws one of
  each at random, so the station no longer plays the same style until you edit
  it. The same genre never comes three times in a row.
- **Song length is random too:** between 2:00 and 4:30 unless you change the
  range (1:00 to 5:00). Both engines used to make songs of about three minutes.
- **Surprise** sets how often a song comes from a genre you did not pick.
  **Shuffle** rolls a whole new mix. A mix can be saved as a **station**; Drift,
  Groove and Explore are built in.
- **Per song:** more like this, less like this (which also skips it), stay on
  this genre, and save. Saved songs are kept and can be played again.
- **Requests** go in one field in the player and show up in the queue.
- A station from v1.6.0 keeps its genres and mood, so it sounds as before until
  you change the mix.

## Dictation

- **Parakeet Ultra** is a second speech engine, chosen on the Dictate page. It
  turns a 3-second clip into text in about 28 ms (Whisper: about 180 ms) and
  hears 25 European languages on its own, but it does not use your vocabulary
  list. Whisper stays the default. If you used Parakeet v3, Ultra is downloaded
  in its place and Whisper handles dictation in the meantime.
- **Speech recognition runs in the engine program**, not in the app. A graphics
  driver crash no longer takes the app or its hotkey down; dictation works again
  about a second later. The app is 61 MB smaller.
- **The recording pill is small again**, the size it had in v1.5.0, with its
  bottom edge where it was.
- **Start at login** writes its entry to your configured config folder
  (`XDG_CONFIG_HOME`).

## Known issues

- On GNOME, the first dictation after the app starts can sometimes paste what
  was on the clipboard before instead of the dictated text (#531). History has
  the right text.
- With smart context on, repeating your previous sentence word for word can
  deliver nothing (#543).
- Switching back to Whisper shows "Preparing GPU (first run only)" every time.
- Speech, cleanup and voice models cannot be deleted from the Models page yet;
  music models can.
